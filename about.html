About Us

SONVEX STUDIO is a creative audio workspace built to give musicians, creators, and audio enthusiasts practical tools in one place.

All tools are free to use.

What we build

Our goal is simple: make useful audio workflows accessible without unnecessary complexity. SONVEX STUDIO brings together tools for lyrics and subtitles, stem work, mastering, audio trimming, track analysis, pitch and tempo adjustment, and audio conversion.

Local-first workflow

Where a tool is designed to run in the browser, processing can happen locally on your device instead of requiring an upload to a server. The exact behavior depends on the individual tool.

Free by design

SONVEX STUDIO is presented as a free workspace. There are no subscription or paid-plan requirements for the tools described on the main studio page.

Our approach

We focus on straightforward interfaces, practical controls, and workflows that let creators get from an idea to a usable audio file with as little friction as possible.
